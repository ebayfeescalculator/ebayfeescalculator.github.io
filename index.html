eBay Fees for Selling in the US — Explained

eBay's fees are calculated based on whether you own a store. If you own a store, costs will vary based on your listed item category. On top of that, eBay rewards Top Rated Sellers with a 10% discount off the final value fee (except in some categories). The discount is sometimes applied at the end of the month on your eBay invoice, so be patient if you don't see it straight away.

There is also a small per-order fee on every sale: $0.30 when the total amount of the sale is $10 or less, and $0.40 when it is over $10. There is a maximum final value fee cap per item sold (not shown in the calculator), which varies depending on the category and your store type.

The final value fee is charged on the total amount of the sale — the item price plus the shipping you charge the buyer, plus any sales tax eBay collects. That is why charging for shipping does not avoid fees, and why this calculator asks for shipping charged separately from your real shipping cost.

Selling a vehicle? Complete vehicles (excluding parts & accessories) use eBay Motors insertion and success fees instead of the standard final value fee, so the rates on this page don't apply. Selling a service? Check eBay's fees for Classified Ads listings.

Sales tax

Since November 2019, eBay has collected sales tax on US sales and remits it to the state — the tax is collected on top of your sold price and does not affect your profit, which is why this calculator shows it separately as "charged to buyer only". Each state charges a different rate, and in some states the tax also applies to shipping & handling charges, so use the "Sales tax includes shipping?" option to match your buyer's state.

International fee

If your buyer's registered address is outside your country, eBay charges an additional 1.65% international fee on the total amount of the sale. Turn on "Overseas sales?" in the calculator to include it.

eBay Promoted Listings

Promoted Listings put your items in more prominent positions across eBay and can increase impressions and sales. You choose the ad rate, which determines the fee charged. The fee is a percentage of the total sale amount (including postage charged and tax), and you are only charged when a buyer clicks on the promoted listing and purchases the item within 30 days. Sellers typically set an ad rate anywhere between 1% and 20%, adjustable in 0.1% steps.

Check eBay's official selling fees page for the latest rates and category details.

How PayPal fees work

When you receive a payment for goods or services, PayPal deducts a percentage of the amount plus a small fixed fee before the money reaches your balance. Personal payments between friends and family in the same country are usually free, but they offer no seller protection and must never be used for sales.

The "Charge this much" figure above reverses the maths: it tells you the gross amount to invoice so that, after PayPal takes its cut, you keep exactly the amount you entered on the right.

About ebayfeescalculator

ebayfeescalculator is a free tool website for online sellers. Our eBay fee calculator works out your final value fee, per-order fee, promoted listings fee and international fee — and, most importantly, your real profit after item and shipping costs — for sales on eBay in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada and Australia.

We built this tool because eBay's fee structure is genuinely hard to read: rates change by category, by store subscription and by seller level, and the fee is charged on the total amount of the sale including shipping and sales tax. A seller who only looks at the headline percentage will misprice items. This calculator does the full maths instantly, as you type, with no sign-up and no data leaving your browser.
